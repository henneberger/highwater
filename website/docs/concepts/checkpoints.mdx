---
title: Checkpoints and replay
description: Bound recovery without putting periodic snapshots on the commit path.
---

Highwater continuously records committed transitions and periodically publishes an immutable checkpoint of materialized state.

A checkpoint contains a consistent progress vector across owned partitions plus state handles. Publication is atomic: recovery sees either the prior complete checkpoint or the new complete checkpoint, never a partially published one.

After publication, durable history covered by the checkpoint becomes eligible for tiering or cleanup. Cleanup never precedes publication.

## Recovery

Recovery:

1. selects the latest compatible published checkpoint;
2. restores or lazily opens its state;
3. validates partition generations;
4. replays every later committed record in order;
5. rebuilds runnable indexes and resumes execution.

An individual transition does not wait for the next checkpoint. Its own durable commit is the acknowledgement boundary. Checkpoints bound replay work, enable partition movement, and lower storage cost.
