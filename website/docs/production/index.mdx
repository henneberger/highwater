---
title: Operate Highwater
description: Understand reliability, recovery, performance, and scale.
---

Highwater is operated around a simple service contract:

- accepted events remain recoverable;
- only current partition owners may commit;
- Process state and emitted output commit atomically;
- backpressure reaches ingestion;
- deployments can move without changing application identity.

Customers operate deployments and service objectives, not storage engines or execution hosts. Use this section to understand what the platform guarantees and how to diagnose application-level symptoms.
