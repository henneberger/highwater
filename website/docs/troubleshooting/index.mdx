---
title: Troubleshoot Highwater
description: Diagnose lag, recovery, retries, and invocation fencing.
---

Start from the visible symptom:

- [Backlog or watermark lag](/troubleshooting/backpressure)
- [Deployment recovering or unavailable](/troubleshooting/recovery)
- [Repeated invocation or fenced completion](/troubleshooting/invocation-leases)

Use `highwater describe DEPLOYMENT` for version and health, `highwater backlogs DEPLOYMENT` for Process and watermark lag, and `highwater logs DEPLOYMENT` for application exceptions.
